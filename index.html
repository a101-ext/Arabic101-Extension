Arabic101 Assistant Extension

Arabic101 Assistant is the official browser extension designed to enhance the productivity of teachers and students at Arabic101 Academy.

App Functionality

  • Chat Notifier: Provides a floating chat widget with sound alerts and desktop notifications so users never miss an important message.
  • Schedule Sync: Allows users to seamlessly sync their Arabic101 class timetable directly to their Google Calendar.

Data Usage & Transparency

To enable the Schedule Sync feature, this extension requests access to your Google Calendar. This access is strictly used to create a dedicated "Arabic101 Academy Schedule" sub-calendar. We use this isolated sub-calendar to inject your class schedule without modifying, reading, or polluting your private personal events.

No personal data or chat history is transmitted to external third-party servers. All data is processed locally within your browser.
