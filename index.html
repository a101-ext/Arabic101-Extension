Arabic101 Assistant

Arabic101 Assistant is the official browser extension designed to enhance the productivity of teachers and students at Arabic101 Academy.

Available on Chrome Web Store

Features

  • Chat Notifier: Real-time chat notifications so you never miss an important message.
  • Schedule Sync: Seamlessly sync your Arabic101 class timetable directly to your Google Calendar.

Data Usage & Transparency

To enable the Schedule Sync feature, this extension requests access to your Google Calendar. This access is strictly used to create and manage a dedicated "Arabic101 Academy Schedule" sub-calendar. We do not read, modify, or delete your personal calendar events.
