Privacy Policy

Arabic101 Assistant v1.1.0.0

Data Collection and Usage

The Arabic101 Assistant extension prioritizes your privacy. We adhere to the following principles:

  • No External Tracking: We do not track, collect, or transmit your personal data, dashboard activities, or chat messages to any third-party servers.
  • Local Storage: All cached data, including your schedules, chat signatures, and preferences, are stored strictly and securely within your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • Direct API Communication: The extension communicates directly with the official dashboard.arabic101.org platform using your active browser session.

Google Calendar Integration

If you choose to sync your schedule with Google Calendar, the extension requires authentication via Google OAuth2. The extension only accesses the minimum scopes necessary to insert and manage Arabic101 schedule events on your behalf. This data is never shared outside of the direct connection between your browser and Google's APIs.
