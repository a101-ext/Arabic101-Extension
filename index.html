Powerful Capabilities

Dual-Calendar Syncing

Features an intelligent architecture that manages your active schedule while automatically migrating past sessions to a dedicated Logs sub-calendar to safely preserve your class history.

Background Auto-Sync

Never miss an update. The extension silently synchronizes your schedule in the background every 5 to 60 minutes based on your current activity, ensuring your Google Calendar is always current.

Bandwidth-Saving Realtime Chat

A beautifully designed chat widget that utilizes a multi-tab leader election algorithm. It ensures only one browser tab communicates with the server, completely eliminating redundant network requests.

Smart Fallback & Offline Mode

If a dashboard profile becomes inactive, the system intelligently reroutes to your secondary active role. You can also export your schedule locally as an .ics file when entirely offline.

How to Get Started

1

Install and Navigate

Download the extension from the Chrome Web Store. Once installed, log in to your Arabic101 dashboard and navigate to your Timetable or Home page.

2

Authorize Google Calendar

Locate the new "Sync Schedule" module on your dashboard. Click Connect Google. During the Google login popup, ensure you manually check the box granting the extension permission to manage your calendar events.

Important Note: Due to Google's strict Granular Consent policies, the calendar access checkbox is left unchecked by default. You must check it manually, otherwise the sync feature will fail.

3

Enable Auto-Sync

Check the Auto-Sync Background toggle. The extension will now silently map your active and completed schedules directly into your Google Calendar without any further input.
