📖 Arabic101 Assistant User Guide

Everything you need to know about setting up, syncing, and maximizing your Arabic101 Extension v1.0.7.1 workflow.

🚀 1. Installation & Companion Modes

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Log in to your Arabic101 Dashboard.
    (Don't have an account yet? Sign up for a class here to unlock your dashboard).
  3. Click the Arabic101 Assistant icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the compact mobile-style popup—or click the Pop-out icon in the header to launch a resizable Standalone Desktop Window with a full sidebar and floating chat widget.
  4. You can also switch between Light Mode and Dark Mode anytime using the theme button in the header.

📅 2. Syncing to Google Calendar

To automatically receive class reminders on your phone or desktop calendar, connect your Google Calendar account:

  1. Click the Google Calendar profile box in the extension header (or Connect Google on the web dashboard) and select Sign in with Google.
  2. Choose the Google Account where you want your class schedule to appear.
  3. Crucial Step: You will see a permission screen asking to manage your calendars. You MUST manually check the box next to "See, edit, share, and permanently delete all the calendars you can access using Google Calendar."
  4. Click Continue. Your connected email address will appear in the header, and the Google Calendar controls will unlock inside the Settings tab.

⚠️ Why manually check the box? Due to Google's strict privacy rules (Granular Consent), calendar permissions are left unchecked by default. If you click Continue without checking the box, the sync will fail to create your Arabic101 calendar.

⚙️ 3. Understanding Calendar Modes

Open the Settings tab in the extension (or click the Gear Icon on the dashboard widget) to choose how your schedule is mapped to your calendar:

🎯 Actual Mode (7 Days or 30 Days)

This mode reads the literal history and future dates of your classes day-by-day. It is highly accurate for real-time changes.

  • It accurately tags past and current classes with statuses like [Canceled], [Absent], [Attended], or [Ongoing].
  • Best for: Teachers and students who want a precise historical log of their day-to-day class attendance.

🔁 Regular (Timetable) Mode

This mode reads your permanent weekly base schedule and creates infinite "recurring weekly events" in Google Calendar.

  • It provides a clean, unchanging overview of your weekly commitments indefinitely into the future.
  • Best for: Users who want to see their general availability without worrying about specific date fluctuations.

🔄 4. Auto-Sync vs Manual Sync

Once connected to Google, you have two ways to keep your calendar updated:

  • Auto-Sync in Background: Toggle this switch ON in Settings. The extension will silently update your Google Calendar in the background (every 15 minutes via background service worker, every 5 minutes when viewing the Timetable page, or every 60 minutes on other dashboard pages).
  • Manual Synchronization: Click Sync Now in the profile menu or in Settings → Google Calendar to push your latest schedule immediately.

💾 5. Offline Mode (Export ICS)

If you prefer not to connect your Google Account, or if you use Apple Calendar or Microsoft Outlook, you can use the offline export feature:

  1. Open the Settings tab and select your desired Calendar Mode.
  2. Click Export ICS under Schedule Settings → General.
  3. A file named Arabic101_actual_7_Date.ics (or matching your chosen mode) will download to your computer.
  4. Open this file with your preferred calendar application to import your classes.

🔔 6. Proactive Class Alerts & Notifications Archive

Arabic101 Assistant actively monitors your upcoming sessions and dashboard alerts even when the portal tab is closed:

  • Class Reminders: Receive desktop notifications 1 hour or 2 hours prior to your scheduled class.
  • Classroom Waiting Alert: Get an urgent desktop banner with a direct Enter Class button as soon as your teacher or student enters the classroom.
  • Schedule Updates: Receive immediate notices if an upcoming session is canceled or adjusted.
  • Notifications Archive: Open the Notifications tab (bell icon) inside the popup to browse paginated alert history, click direct invoice payment links, or mark all alerts as read.

💬 7. Realtime Chat & Teacher Workflow Tools

  • Popup Messenger & Floating Widget: Reply to messages directly from the Chat / Messages tab in popup view, or use the floating chat dock in the bottom-right corner on wide desktop screens.
  • Bandwidth Saver: Multi-tab leader election ensures only ONE active browser context polls the chat server at a time, saving RAM and network bandwidth.
  • Teacher Classroom Actions: Teachers can view unclosed Yesterday's Sessions, send classroom reminders, mark waiting classes as Running, submit End Class reports (with automatic draft recovery), and manage weekly Availability slots directly from the extension.

🛠️ 8. Troubleshooting & Reset

If the extension behaves unexpectedly or Google Calendar sync fails:

  • Clear & Re-Sync Calendar: Open Settings → Delete Arabic101 GCal to safely remove the active Arabic101 Academy Schedule sub-calendar from your Google account and start fresh. Note: Your past classes archived in the Logs calendar remain preserved!
  • Re-authorize Google Permissions: Click your profile box in the header, choose Sign out Google, and sign in again—making sure to manually check the Google Calendar access box.
  • Refresh Assistant: Click the Arabic101 logo in the top-left header (or drawer header) at any time to refresh the extension view.
